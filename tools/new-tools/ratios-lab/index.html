Using Ratios to Determine the Scale Factor Between Scaled Copies

▶ Lesson video

Controls

Scale factor k
Vertical factor

Key Idea

A scaled copy multiplies every length of the original by the same number, the scale factor.

scale factor = copy length ÷ original length

k > 1 enlarges, k < 1 shrinks, and k = 1 keeps the same size. Angles never change.

Area is multiplied by k², not k.

Key Idea

A scale like 1 cm : 2 m is a ratio. Every 1 cm on the drawing stands for 2 m in real life.

1 cm represents (actual ÷ drawing)

To get an actual length, multiply the drawing length by the scale. To get a drawing length, divide.

Key Idea

In a proportional relationship, y ÷ x is the same for every row. That number is the constant of proportionality, k.

k = y ÷ x

k is also the unit rate: the value of y when x = 1.
