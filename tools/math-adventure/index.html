📋 For parents & tutors: how to use this

Each topic starts with a picture tool, then practice problems. A student earns a ⭐ for each problem solved on the first try without a hint. Aim for 5 stars in a topic before moving on. Tutors can set the focus areas for each student under Plan in AMtutorTools.

WeekFocus (15–20 minutes a day)

4🥕 Line Plots, 📦 Volume, 🧱 Combining Volumes, plus one review topic each day

Tips: Use each picture tool with her before practice and ask her to explain what she sees. Have her say the steps out loud. Aim for 5 stars per topic before moving on. Stars are saved in this browser only.
